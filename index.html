Our heritage

Baking on this street since 1597

Few bakeries anywhere can claim four centuries of daily baking. Ours can. The ovens, the recipes and the early mornings have been handed down through generations of Plymouth bakers.

  • 1597

    The bakery begins its life on Southside Street — before the Mayflower ever sailed.

  • World Bread Awards

    Our loaves have taken gold at the World Bread Awards — twice.

  • Today

    Still a working bakery first: bread out of the oven every morning, deli counter alongside.

Today's bakes

Bakes worth crossing the Barbican for

Everything is baked on site, in small daily batches — when it's gone, it's gone. Three things regulars cross the Barbican for:

Signature sourdough

Slow-fermented and wood-fired, with deep flavour and a proper crust — the loaf the bakery is known for.

Shaped by hand, daily

Morning rolls, buns and butter pastries are shaped and baked on site in small daily batches — when it's gone, it's gone.

The delicatessen counter

Artisan cheeses, cured meats sliced to order, and preserves and chutneys chosen to go with the bread.
